
MotorShield AI Architecture:

The CNN Layer detects local patterns like commutation spikes in motor currents and operates with high speed and efficiency, but struggles with long-range dependencies across the entire signal.

The Transformer Layer captures global relationships across time and understands complex temporal dependencies, but requires more computational resources compared to CNNs.

We chose the Hybrid Solution because CNN alone cannot capture global context, while Transformer alone is computationally expensive for real-time applications. The hybrid approach uses CNN to extract local features, which then feed into the Transformer to analyze global context, combining efficiency with comprehensive understanding.

The Processing pipeline transforms three-phase currents through calibration and filtering before feeding into the hybrid model for fault detection.

The Results show detection of four fault conditions - healthy operation, open circuits, inter-turn shorts, and switch failures - with 95% plus accuracy.

The Impact is preventing costly failures through real-time predictive maintenance.
