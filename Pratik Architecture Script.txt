
CNN Analysis:
  - Excel at detecting local temporal patterns like commutation spikes and short-duration anomalies
  - Computationally efficient and robust to noise
  - Limitation: Struggle with long-range dependencies across entire signal window

Transformer Analysis:
  - Self-attention mechanisms capture global context and relationships across entire time series
  - Excellent at understanding complex temporal patterns and dependencies
  - Limitation: Require more data and computational resources compared to CNNs

Hybrid Architecture Solution:
  - CNNs extract local features first, identifying critical spikes and local patterns in three-phase motor currents
  - Transformers analyze relationships between features across time, capturing broader context and long-range dependencies
  - Combines efficiency and local pattern recognition of CNNs with global context understanding of Transformers

Processing Pipeline:
  - Three-phase motor current signals processed through sophisticated preprocessing chain
  - Calibration, filtering, and windowing before feeding into hybrid model

Results:
  - Detect four critical fault conditions: healthy operation, open circuits, inter-turn shorts, control switch failures
  - Achieve 95% plus accuracy

Impact:
  - MotorShield AI represents the future of predictive maintenance
  - Prevents costly equipment failures through intelligent, real-time motor health monitoring
