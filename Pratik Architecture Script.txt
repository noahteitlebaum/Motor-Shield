
CNN Analysis:
  CNNs excel at detecting local temporal patterns like commutation spikes and short-duration anomalies.
  They are computationally efficient and robust to noise.
  Their limitation is that they struggle with long-range dependencies across the entire signal window.

Transformer Analysis:
  Transformers use self-attention mechanisms that capture global context and relationships across the entire time series.
  They are excellent at understanding complex temporal patterns and dependencies.
  Their limitation is that they require more data and computational resources compared to CNNs.

Hybrid Architecture Solution:
  CNNs extract local features first, identifying critical spikes and local patterns in three-phase motor currents.
  Transformers then analyze relationships between features across time, capturing broader context and long-range dependencies.
  This approach combines the efficiency and local pattern recognition of CNNs with the global context understanding of Transformers.

Processing Pipeline:
  Three-phase motor current signals are processed through a sophisticated preprocessing chain.
  The signals undergo calibration, filtering, and windowing before feeding into the hybrid model.

Results:
  The system detects four critical fault conditions: healthy operation, open circuits, inter-turn shorts, and control switch failures.
  It achieves 95% plus accuracy.

Impact:
  MotorShield AI represents the future of predictive maintenance.
  It prevents costly equipment failures through intelligent, real-time motor health monitoring.
